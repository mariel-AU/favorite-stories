What these stories did to me

What did these stories change in me?

These stories changed how I look at people and at my own choices. Titanic reminds me that time with the people I love is limited, Swapped taught me to listen before I judge, and Bet showed me how pressure can push people into decisions that are hard to undo. Each one left me with an idea I still think about.

Which scene, character, or idea stays with me the most?

The idea that stays with me most comes from Titanic: the people we love are only here for a limited time. I think about it whenever I’m tempted to put off saying something that matters.

How do I want you to feel?

I want you to leave this site a little quieter than you arrived, and curious about the stories in your own life.

Three things that stay with me

Time is not promised

Theme from Titanic

Titanic reminds me that the people we love are not here forever. It pushes me to say what I mean today instead of waiting for a better moment.

Notice the small things

Theme from My Neighbor Totoro

Totoro is about stopping to notice small things like rain, a bus stop, and a quiet forest. It reminds me that wonder does not need a big event.

Risky choices have a price

Theme from Bet

Bet shows how the pressure to win can make people take risks they cannot take back. It makes me stop and ask what a choice could cost before I make it.
